How it is wired: PIC-100 holds steam pressure by trimming the burner on top of a steam-demand feedforward. LIC-200 and FIC-201 form a three-element drum level control: the level controller adds a correction to the measured steam flow, and that becomes the feedwater flow setpoint. LIC-300 keeps the deaerator level with makeup water, and PIC-301 holds deaerator pressure with pegging steam. Switch any loop to MAN to drive its output yourself and see what the rest of the plant does.
